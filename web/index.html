Bambuddy Spool Scanner

Flash the ESP32 + RC522 spool scanner straight from your browser. Hold a Bambu Lab spool, or its unopened box, over the reader and it lands in your Bambuddy inventory.

Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Flashing needs a secure (https) page.

Firmware …

1. Connect

  1. Wire the RC522 to the ESP32 as shown in the wiring guide.
  2. Plug the ESP32 into this computer with a USB data cable.
  3. Click Connect above and pick the ESP32's serial port. If none shows up, install the CP210x or CH340 USB driver for your board.

2. Set up

  1. In Bambuddy, create an API key with the Manage inventory permission.
  2. After flashing, join the WiFi network SpoolScanner-Setup with your phone.
  3. Pick your WiFi and enter the Bambuddy URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:8000) and the API key.
  4. To change settings later, hold the BOOT button for 3 seconds.

3. Scan

One long blink: spool added. Two short blinks: already in Bambuddy. Five fast blinks: error (see the serial log).
